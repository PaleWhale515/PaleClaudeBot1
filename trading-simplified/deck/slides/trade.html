This is the core innovation. Instead of an options chain, the customer answers three questions: direction, expiration, and chance of profit. The engine picks the strikes. The trade-off is always visible: a higher chance of profit always means a smaller payout for the risk, so "make" and "lose" sit side by side at equal size. It never sells uncovered options. The stop-loss is mandatory: half the premium for bought options, twice the credit for sold spreads, never beyond the maximum loss; it fills at the next available price. Single calls and puts work in a cash account after options approval; the 50–99% side sells spreads and needs margin (Tier B). Each trade is the customer's own order through the partner; we don't hold the options or issue tokens. The Detailed view shows legs, IV and Greeks for experienced users.
