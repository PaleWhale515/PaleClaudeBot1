Balance alone never upgrades anyone. The discipline check covers Smart Stake adherence, planned exits and drawdown. When a user qualifies, we recommend them; the partner approves under its own process, including FINRA Rule 4210's $2,000 margin minimum and Rule 2360 for options. Margin accounts follow FINRA's intraday margin standards, which replaced the pattern-day-trader rule on June 4, 2026 (firms may phase them in through October 20, 2027), so there is no 3-day-trade cap for Tier B. If the balance later falls, limits drop back automatically. Tier C prediction sizing is still to be decided.
