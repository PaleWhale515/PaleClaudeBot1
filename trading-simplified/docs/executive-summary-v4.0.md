# Trading Simplified Executive Summary

Sep 27, 2026 · Live version: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/c9fd8d48-951d-4b4d-9c5d-50949b197ecb

Trading Simplified takes first-time traders from a $20 prediction to a real options account, and only lets them reach margin once their habits prove they're ready. It is a non-custodial app built to be acquired by an exchange or broker.

## What it is

- **Predict:** one tap on UP or DOWN buys a fixed $20 stake in a short-horizon event contract on a regulated exchange.
- **Trade:** the Probability Slider. The customer picks a direction, an expiration (today to 1 year) and a chance of profit (1% to 99%); the engine picks the SPY options strikes. Every trade has a fixed maximum loss and a stop-loss the customer can't remove.
- **Graduate:** margin unlocks only with a $2,000 balance, a Discipline Score above 85 and the broker's approval.

## Why it is worth buying

- **Safer customers from day one:** Smart Stake caps new risk at 25% of equity, every options trade carries a system-enforced stop-loss, and trading pauses automatically when broker latency passes 500 ms.
- **A discipline record:** a five-factor Discipline Score (sizing, planned exits, drawdown, frequency, consistency) documents every customer who reaches margin.
- **Built for compliance review:** defined-risk trades only, no custody, no tokens, and each order is the customer's own. The design follows the 2026 FINRA intraday margin rules that replaced the pattern-day-trader rule.
- **Ready to integrate:** a working prototype of the full customer flow, ready to sit on a buyer's execution, custody and approval systems.

## Revenue

| Stream | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Per-contract commission | A micro-fee per side on every contract executed ($0.01 in the prototype) |
| Order-routing revenue | A disclosed share of payment for order flow on SPY options orders |
| Plus, $9.99 per month | Instant deposits, reduced commissions, an automated trading journal and Seasonal Ladders |

Revenue grows with funded accounts and graduation to options, not with how often any one customer trades.
